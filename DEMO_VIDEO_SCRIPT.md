# MANSI — 2.5 Minute Demo Video Script & Walkthrough
## Code to Connect: Women in Tech Hackathon 2026
**Presenter:** Mansi Singh (Team Lead, B.Tech Final Year, ABV-IIITM Gwalior)  
**Track:** Track 1 — Connect Online  
**Target Duration:** 2 minutes 45 seconds *(Max allowable: 3:00 minutes)*

```
[0:00 - 0:30] 🎬 Hook & Problem: Academic Isolation & The Stigma Wall
[0:30 - 0:55] 💡 Solution Overview: What is MANSI?
```

### 🎬 Scene 1: The Hook & Inspiration (0:00 – 0:30)
**On Screen:** Mansi on camera or intro title card with MANSI logo + *“Code to Connect Hackathon 2026”*. Transition to high-pressure university campus statistics.

> **Mansi (Voiceover):**  
> *"Hi everyone, I'm Mansi Singh, a final-year B.Tech student at ABV-IIITM Gwalior, and team lead for **MANSI**.*  
> *In rigorous academic environments, over 70% of students facing severe burnout and emotional distress suffer in silence without ever reaching out. The problem isn't a lack of counselors—it’s the paralyzing fear of social stigma, privacy leaks, and clunky institutional barriers that make students feel digitally and emotionally isolated.*  
> *For the **Code to Connect Hackathon**, we asked: How can we build an empathetic, privacy-first bridge that connects students to genuine support without ever putting their identity at risk? That is why we built **MANSI**—Mental Awareness, Nurturing & Support Interface."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 2: Solution Overview (0:30 – 0:55)
**On Screen:** MANSI Homepage / Landing page showing the clean, calming pastel aesthetic, key role buttons (Student, Psychologist, Teacher, Institution).

> **Mansi (Voiceover):**  
> *"MANSI is a multi-stakeholder mental wellness platform that bridges the gap between four key campus groups: **Students**, **Certified Counselors**, **Faculty Teachers**, and **University Administrators**.*  
> *Built with Next.js 14 and MongoDB, MANSI provides end-to-end anonymity for students while giving institutions aggregated wellbeing intelligence through zero-knowledge data visualization."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 3: Live Walkthrough (0:55 – 1:45)
**On Screen:** Screen recording of the live app in action.

#### 1. Student Portal (`/student`)
> **Mansi (Voiceover):**  
> *"Let's log in as a student. Notice that upon signing in, my personal identity is replaced with an ephemeral, cryptographic alias.*  
> *With our 1-tap **'Instant Safe Connect'**, I can immediately launch a confidential encrypted chat session with a campus psychologist. I can also complete a quick 30-second **Wellness Assessment** that maps my stress levels against my upcoming academic deadlines."*

#### 2. Psychologist Portal (`/psychologist`)
> **Mansi (Voiceover):**  
> *"Switching to the Counselor portal: Certified psychologists see a prioritized triage inbox of incoming anonymous sessions. They can chat in real-time, provide empathetic grounding, and tag session categories like 'Exam Anxiety' or 'Social Isolation'—all without ever knowing the student's real name."*

#### 3. Institutional Analytics (`/dashboard`)
> **Mansi (Voiceover):**  
> *"Finally, on the University Admin dashboard, leaders can inspect interactive **Recharts** analytics—tracking campus-wide stress trends across departments and exam weeks. Crucially, the mathematical separation in our database prevents anyone from ever tracing data back to an individual student."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 4: What We Changed — Mentor Iteration (1:45 – 2:15)
**On Screen:** Side-by-side mockup showing "Before Mentor Check-in (6-step intake form)" vs. "After Mentor Check-in (1-tap Instant Safe Connect)".

> **Mansi (Voiceover):**  
> *"A pivotal moment during our hackathon journey was our Saturday mentor session.*  
> *Our mentor pointed out that our initial prototype forced distressed students through a 6-step questionnaire before they could even talk to a human, causing friction and drop-offs during acute anxiety moments.*  
> *We immediately listened and refactored our pipeline: we built a 1-tap Instant Connect launcher on the home screen and turned the questionnaire into an optional post-reflection step. This slashed our time-to-first-connection from over 100 seconds to under 8 seconds."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 5: Tech Stack & Architecture (2:15 – 2:35)
**On Screen:** Architectural diagram showing Next.js 14 App Router, Serverless APIs, MongoDB Atlas, JWT/Bcrypt security layer, and Recharts.

> **Mansi (Voiceover):**  
> *"Under the hood, MANSI is engineered with Next.js 14, React 18, Tailwind CSS, and MongoDB with Mongoose. Authentication is completely stateless using JWTs and secure HTTP cookies, backed by CSV roster parsing via PapaParse for institutional whitelisting."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 6: Conclusion & Impact (2:35 – 2:45)
**On Screen:** Mansi on camera or closing slide with GitHub Repo & Live Vercel Demo link.

> **Mansi (Voiceover):**  
> *"MANSI proves that code can break stigma, protect privacy, and build meaningful emotional connections across academic communities.*  
> *Thank you for watching, and thank you to the Women in Tech team for hosting Code to Connect!"*

### 💡 Tips for Recording:
1. **Screen Resolution:** Record at 1080p (1920x1080) with a clean browser window.
2. **Audio:** Use headphones with a clear mic, minimizing background noise.
3. **Pacing:** Speak clearly and confidently; the 2:45 timing leaves a 15-second buffer before the 3:00 cutoff.
4. **Upload:** Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** and paste the link into Devpost.
