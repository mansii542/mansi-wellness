### 📊 Slide 2: The Problem (The Stigma & Isolation Wall)
- **70%+ of students** facing severe anxiety and burnout never seek counseling support.
- **3 Major Blockers:**
  1. **Social Stigma:** Fear of being judged by peers and professors if seen visiting a counselor.
  2. **Reactive, Not Proactive:** Counselors only intervene when a full crisis occurs.
  3. **Institutional Blindspots:** Universities lack privacy-preserving wellness intelligence.

---

### 📊 Slide 3: The Solution (MANSI's 4-Role Connected Network)
- **Student Portal:** Ephemeral identity, 1-tap confidential chat with certified counselors, guided wellness check-ins.
- **Psychologist Portal:** Prioritized triage inbox, anonymous session tagging, empathetic intervention.
- **Teacher Portal:** Classroom wellbeing pulse and academic stress drop correlation.
- **Institutional Admin:** Zero-knowledge aggregate stress radar and semester wellness trend analytics.

---

### 📊 Slide 4: Key Technical Innovations & Privacy Architecture
- **Zero-Knowledge Decoupling:** Complete separation between academic student records and therapy chat sessions.
- **Tech Stack:** Next.js 14 (App Router), React 18, Tailwind CSS, Recharts for dynamic visual analytics.
- **Backend & Database:** MongoDB with Mongoose ODM, Stateless JWT Auth, Bcryptjs encryption, CSV PapaParse ingestion.

---

### 📊 Slide 5: Mentor Iteration ("What We Changed" - Scored Criterion)
- **Before Check-in:** 6-step intake questionnaire before starting chat (90+ sec friction).
- **Mentor Feedback:** Distressed students experience drop-off when forced into multi-step forms during acute anxiety.
- **After Pivot:** 1-tap **"Instant Safe Connect"** launcher; detailed questionnaires moved to optional post-session reflections.
- **Result:** Reduced time-to-first-connection by **91%** (under 8 seconds).
